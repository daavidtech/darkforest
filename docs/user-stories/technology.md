# Technology

## View Technology State

As a player, I can view known technologies so that I understand what my
civilization can currently do.

Acceptance details:

- The technology page shows current broad technology stage.
- The technology page shows known discoveries.
- The technology page shows available research directions.
- Hidden technologies are not fully revealed before discovery.
- Recent discoveries are linked to mailbox reports.

## Discover Technology

As a player, my civilization can discover technology so that innovation creates
asymmetric progress.

Acceptance details:

- Discovery depends on requirements, innovation score, buildings, specialists,
  resources, and events.
- Some discoveries are predictable unlocks.
- Some discoveries are probabilistic breakthroughs.
- A discovery unlocks new buildings, units, effects, or research directions.
- A discovery report is added to the mailbox.
